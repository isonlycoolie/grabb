# Code of Conduct

grabb follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, never about the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing others' private
  information.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any place
where you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@isonlycoolie](https://github.com/isonlycoolie). Reports are handled confidentially, and
the maintainer may warn, suspend or ban participants who break these rules, as set out in
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
